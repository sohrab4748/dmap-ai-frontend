DMAP-AI platform structure

DMAP-AI is organized as a growing platform. The Research Version is available first, paid research workflows are the next step, and the Farmer Version will extend the system toward practical agricultural decision support.

Research Version

Free and paid plans

The Research Version is the main scientific workflow of DMAP-AI. It provides a focused workflow for learning, research, consulting, and point-based drought assessment.

  • • SPI analysis for a selected point
  • • NASA POWER as the default free data source
  • • ERA5-Land/CDS as an advanced option
  • • Tables, charts, severity summaries, wavelet diagnostics, copula diagnostics, and saved runs

Farmer Version (FV)

Platform expansion

FV will expand DMAP-AI into agricultural drought communication, with emphasis on crop stage, short-term risk, and practical explanations for decision-makers.

  • • Crop-aware drought-risk summaries
  • • Corn and soybean growing-season context
  • • Short-term moisture-risk outlooks for the next 2–4 weeks
  • • Advisor-friendly explanations for producers and decision-makers

How DMAP-AI works

The same basic workflow supports the current Research Version and the planned research and farmer-facing versions.

1

Select a location

Choose a point in the current Research Version. Future workflows can extend the same structure to regional and field-level analysis.

2

Choose data and method

Run SPI with NASA POWER by default or ERA5-Land/CDS as an advanced option. Licensed workflows will add more drought indices and data sources.

3

Review and communicate results

Review tables, charts, severity metrics, wavelet and copula diagnostics, save runs, and prepare outputs for research or decision support.
