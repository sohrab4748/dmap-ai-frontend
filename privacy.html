Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AgriMetSoft LLC ("AgriMetSoft," "we," "our," or "us") handles information when you use DMAP-AI, including the DMAP-AI website, the DMAP-AI Research Version, and the DMAP-AI ChatGPT app or connector.

DMAP-AI is a drought analysis tool. It processes location and analysis settings selected by the user to generate SPI tables, drought severity events, wavelet scalogram summaries, and drought periodicity diagnostics.

Information we collect or process

Depending on how you use DMAP-AI, we may process the following information:

  • Location inputs, such as latitude and longitude, map point, or selected region.
  • Analysis settings, such as date range, baseline period, data source, SPI scale, aggregation window, drought threshold, and category method.
  • Generated drought analysis outputs, such as SPI values, category labels, drought severity events, wavelet scalogram summaries, global wavelet spectrum, and periodicity results.
  • Account information for signed-in users, such as email address, profile identifier, plan status, saved runs, and usage limits.
  • Technical information, such as browser type, device information, IP address, timestamps, server logs, error logs, and security/rate-limit logs.
  • Messages or prompts you submit to the DMAP-AI ChatGPT app, to the extent needed to run the requested drought analysis.

How we use information

  • To run requested drought analyses and return selected outputs.
  • To operate, maintain, debug, and secure DMAP-AI services.
  • To save and reload user runs when that feature is enabled.
  • To apply access limits, plan rules, and anti-abuse protections.
  • To improve the reliability, usability, and documentation of DMAP-AI.
  • To respond to support requests and communicate about the service.

ChatGPT app and third-party services

When you use the DMAP-AI ChatGPT app, ChatGPT may send your request and analysis parameters to the DMAP-AI MCP server so the requested tool can run. DMAP-AI returns selected results to ChatGPT for display. We do not sell user data to advertisers.

DMAP-AI may use or reference third-party services for data, authentication, hosting, analytics, and infrastructure. These may include NASA POWER, ERA5/Copernicus or ECMWF-related data services, Auth0 or Google sign-in, cloud hosting providers, and OpenAI/ChatGPT when you use the ChatGPT app.

Cookies and analytics

DMAP-AI may use cookies, local storage, or analytics tools to support sign-in, saved runs, usage measurement, security, and website performance. You can control cookies through your browser settings, but some features may not work correctly if cookies or local storage are disabled.

Data retention

We retain information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, including operating the service, maintaining saved runs, meeting legal obligations, resolving disputes, and enforcing our terms. Server logs and security records may be retained for a limited period for reliability and abuse prevention.

Data sharing

We may share information with service providers that help us operate DMAP-AI, such as hosting, authentication, analytics, support, and data-processing providers. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect our rights, to prevent abuse, or in connection with a business transfer.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect DMAP-AI systems. No online service can guarantee complete security. Users are responsible for protecting their account credentials and for using DMAP-AI responsibly.

Your choices

You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information associated with your DMAP-AI account, subject to legal, security, and operational limitations. You may also stop using DMAP-AI at any time.

Children

DMAP-AI is intended for research, educational, professional, and informational use. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
